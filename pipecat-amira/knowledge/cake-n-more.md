Source: https://cakenmore.com/

## Company

Cake N More is a UAE-wide cake, dessert, and bakery delivery brand. It sells made-to-order cakes and desserts and emphasizes fresh ingredients, custom designs, and reliable delivery for celebrations. Its tagline is “Cake N More — one bite, and you'll want more!”
